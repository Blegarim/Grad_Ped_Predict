# Multi-seed plan — converting the thesis result into a defensible paper

**Written 2026-09-21.** Deadline extension available (supervisor is on the committee), so the constraint is

## What must be repeated, and why only these

The claim is *pure hazard training beats binary training on detection latency*. That needs **two** arms at
**three** seeds. Everything else is supporting and can stay at n=1 with a stated limitation.

| arm | seeds | why |
|---|---|---|
| **R3 `onset_pure`** | 42 ✅, **43, 44** | the claim |
| **R2s `pose_baseline` streaming** | **42, 43, 44** | the TRUE binary baseline — currently proxied by R1's `crosses_frame` |
| R4 `onset_hedge` | 42 ✅ only | negative control; "mixing hurts" survives at n=1 as a stated limitation |
| R3C `onset_pure_censored` | 42 ✅ only | secondary trade result; same |

**Why R2s and not R1 as the baseline.** R1 is the *auxiliary* arm — its `crosses_frame` was trained
alongside a hazard term at weight 0.1. Using it as "the binary baseline" is a proxy a reviewer will
challenge. R2s is the same-machine, no-onset, crosses-only streaming baseline: the honest comparator.

## Run order — interleaved so a matched pair completes early

Ordered so that if the box is lost at any point, we always hold *matched* seeds rather than three of one
arm and none of the other:

1. `r2s_train` seed 42 — already queued; unlocks the true-baseline curve
2. `r3_s43`
3. `r2s_s43` ← after this, a 2-seed matched comparison exists
4. `r3_s44`
5. `r2s_s44` ← 3-seed matched comparison

## Reporting protocol (decide NOW, before seeing the numbers)

Pre-registering this so the analysis cannot drift after the fact — which matters especially given gap 3
below.

- **Primary:** detection rate at fixed false-alarm budgets {1200, 460, 205, 95, 41}/hr, **mean ± sd over 3
  seeds**, R3 vs R2s.
- **Secondary:** lead time at the same budgets, same aggregation.
- **Reported alongside, not instead:** window AUC and val-tuned F1, to show the arms tie there.
- **Success criterion, stated in advance:** R3's mean detection rate exceeds R2s's by more than the sum of
  their sds at ≥3 of the 5 budgets. Anything less is reported as inconclusive, not as a win.

## The one gap that cannot be closed by running more

**The metric was chosen after observing that the arms tied on F1.** That is structurally how spurious
findings arise, and no amount of compute repairs it. What makes it defensible:

- the metric is **independently motivated** — it is how a deployed warning system is specified, and it
  comes from a 70-year-old field (quickest change detection) rather than being invented here;
- it was picked for a **mechanistic reason** stated in advance of measuring: sequential detection needs an
  accumulating statistic, which a hazard model provides and a fixed-horizon classifier does not;
- **it is now pre-registered** for the seed runs above, which are genuine out-of-sample confirmation.

This must be written **explicitly in the paper**, not buried. A reviewer who spots it unaided will
discount the whole result; a paper that raises and answers it first is far stronger.
